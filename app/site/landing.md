# Pianola

**Pianola** turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a tap-played modern player grand piano. Pick any of **87 classic piano rolls** and **tap any key** to conduct every chord and phrase with your own timing and touch on a dual-core physical-modal concert grand — or tap `ARP` anytime to break out into a **Basic 27-key Grand Piano**, **Chord Mode**, and a **4-slot Roll Recorder**.

![Pianola in Tap-Play mode on the FM-1 screen](manual/img/scene_pianist.png "Pianola on the FM-1 screen")

This is version **{{VERSION}}**. Installing custom firmware is at your own risk; the Web MIDI installer can return your unit to M-VAVE's official firmware at any time.
